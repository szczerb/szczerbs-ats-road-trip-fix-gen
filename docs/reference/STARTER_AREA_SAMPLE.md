## Why the city station was left unchanged

**Observed locally:** the city truck control listed above was recognized, approximately 344 map units from the garage. Its v25 descriptor has four GAS locators and four linked services, all truck-oriented by the inspected flags. The initial generator supports only a reviewed `02003` single-locator/single-service shape. That deliberately narrow scope excluded the nearer multi-point station; no old-format incompatibility or inherent conversion hazard was diagnosed. See the [selection review](VALIDATION_RESULTS.md#why-the-closer-city-station-was-not-selected).

The user's first test reports successful refueling at the eastern target but a substantial driving detour. Straight-line distance did not establish a convenient road approach. Before broadening to the city station, establish how to add car support while preserving truck service at the same physical pump. Logical locator/service matching is necessary for scoped edits; it does not imply that physical dispensers must be divided between cars and trucks. The screenshots do not locate each visible forecourt dispenser in world coordinates, so visual pump-to-locator association remains to be checked.

## Additional fuel evidence and sample changes

**Observed locally:** among recognized station-prefab instances in the 270 sectors, 448 diesel-only descriptors had 813 linked services, all with flags `0x80000000`. Another 119 prefabs whose descriptors include GAS flags `1` had 254 services with flags `0x80004000` and 28 remaining flags-`0x80000000` services in mixed stations. Visual-only roofs with no GAS locators were excluded from those counts. All 220 instances of the selected single-GAS `02003` model had one flags-0 GAS locator and one flags-`0x80000000` service. Counts are for matched resources, not every fuel interaction in the complete game.

**Hypothesis, strong local evidence:** the service bit `0x4000`, together with PPD GAS flags `1`, represents car/petrol refueling. [ServiceSerializer](https://github.com/sk-zk/TruckLib/blob/master/TruckLib/ScsMap/Serialization/ServiceSerializer.cs), [MapItemSerializer](https://github.com/sk-zk/TruckLib/blob/master/TruckLib/ScsMap/Serialization/MapItemSerializer.cs), and [PrefabSerializer](https://github.com/sk-zk/TruckLib/blob/master/TruckLib/ScsMap/Serialization/PrefabSerializer.cs) support the field offsets, not this bit's runtime meaning. This extends the [earlier descriptor research](REFUELING_RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md); it is not an official complete flag specification.

The sample contains three gameplay resources:

1. `def/world/prefab.rtfe9bc1db8.sii`: additive prefab alias preserving all supported stock `02003` settings except its identifier and descriptor path; existing stock definitions remain untouched.
2. `prefab/rtfix/rtfe9bc1db8.ppd`: 908-byte private descriptor copy; only byte 336 changes from `0` to `1`. It retains the same single GAS locator, transform, nodes, and section layout.
3. `map/usa/sec-0016-0014.base`: same-size sector override; target instance points to the private alias and its existing service gains `0x4000`. Exactly seven bytes differ from the supplied sector: offsets 79427, 79428, 79429, 79430, 79431, 79433, and 207355.

**Observed locally:** no locator, node, service, UID, or item count is added. Existing `.data` and other sector companions remain supplied by ATS Expansion. This avoids the old experiment's added-locator versus existing-service count mismatch. It does not prove that all runtime caches or service semantics are understood.

**User-reported follow-up:** with the Helena-area sample enabled, the converted point is visible only in car-map mode and disappears from truck-map mode; the earlier truck screenshot predates installation. Actual post-patch truck refueling is still untested. See [corrected test context](VALIDATION_RESULTS.md#follow-up-correction-patched-truck-map-visibility). **Original expected behavior:** one existing pump becomes car-oriented; truck use at **that pump may disappear**. Nearby truck pumps retain their data. This sample intentionally tests conversion of one existing interaction; it does not add dual car/truck access at the same spot.

**Compatibility boundary:** a `.base` override replaces the **whole sector** in resource resolution, even though seven bytes change. Use ATS Expansion 23.0 and the inspected game baseline, with the sample above it. Another map/patch changing this sector or its companion data can conflict. The generator's source hashes protect generation inputs; they cannot enforce the eventual game's mod order. Keep the original mod's DLC requirements; this experiment additionally requires Road Trip Ford. Keep copied game/mod assets out of tracked project files; this generated sample remains a local artifact.
