# Publication boundary for the North America Background navigation overlay

**Inspected 2026-10-23.** This note concerns North America Background Map **5.3** (Steam Workshop item [3348711188](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3348711188), shown as created by CairamR; its installed manifest names author “CAIRAM”) and the project's N2 NAV PARKING + FUEL **r2** experimental overlay for ATS 1.61.3.18. The [r3 presentation package](NAVIGATION_ICONS_METADATA_1_61_R3.md) retains that gameplay member byte-for-byte, verified on 2026-10-23. This is a source and publication assessment, not a legal determination or authorization to release either package.

## Contents and demonstrated behavior

**Observed locally — 2026-10-19 and checked 2026-10-23:** N2's only gameplay member is a complete **3,236-byte** replacement of the background's def/map_data.sii. It changes four local map/minimap tier mask values (indices 0–3) from 0x00685FFF to 0x03685FFF, enabling category bits **24** (fuel) and **25** (car parking). All other source bytes, including world-map tiers 4–7, are unchanged. Reversing those four token edits recovers the original definition. The three referenced SUI include files remain in the installed background and are not bundled in N2; it also bundles no background map textures or map sectors. The generator pins the source definition and three includes to the reviewed 5.3 hashes and rejects drift. See the [N2 payload audit and user-validation scope](CAR_PARKING_NAVIGATION_ICONS.md#n2-analogous-fuel-visibility-candidate).

The mask change affects global marker visibility at those tiers and may reveal existing fuel and parking markers throughout the map. It creates no markers, parking geometry, rest or fuel services, prices, or economy changes. **User-reported — 2026-10-19:** both icons were visible in Mustang navigation in the tested context; the exact surface, location, zoom and wider coverage were not reported. **Observed locally — r10 stack, 2026-10-21:** Reforma OtherMaps Compatibility Patch 40.161 also supplies def/map_data.sii, so N2 (and a payload-identical r3) must load above **every** map-data provider, including that patch and the background. [Exact stack audit](REALISTIC_MAP_REPLACEMENTS_1_61_R10.md).

## Rights evidence and limits

**Documented — inspected 2026-10-23:** The [Workshop listing](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3348711188) describes CairamR's background and links another author mod, but the visible description states no license or permission to redistribute modified definition files. The installed 5.3 manifest identifies CAIRAM; its description gives usage and load-order text. A targeted scan of the installed universal package found no license, permission, or credit file. Absence of a found grant does **not** establish who owns every byte, prove infringement, or rule out permission outside the inspected sources. The Workshop page also renders a generic removed/incompatible banner alongside ordinary item controls; that display alone does not establish the item's actual availability or rights status.

**Documented — Valve, [Steam Subscriber Agreement §§2 and 6](https://store.steampowered.com/subscriber_agreement/), inspected 2026-10-23:** subscribed content is licensed for personal use subject to stated exceptions. Workshop contributions may be based on a supported game, but the uploader warrants sufficient rights in the uploaded content and original creation or contributor authorization. Uploading to Steam requires granting Valve rights; subscribing to CairamR's item or naming it as a dependency does not itself supply a general license to redistribute its modified file. App-specific and destination-host terms may also apply.

**Documented — SCS, [2019 modding clarification](https://forum.scssoft.com/viewtopic.php?t=270259):** SCS staff accepted an example of a DLC-dependent, art-free edit to **SCS-owned** definitions. That example supports a limited reading for SCS assets; it does not grant rights in CairamR's third-party definition. The project's [SCS publication review](SCS_MOD_PUBLICATION_TERMS.md) likewise explicitly excludes third-party map files.

**Assessment:** although the functional edit is only four tokens and no SUI files or textures are copied, the distributable N2 archive contains the complete modified background definition. Permission to publish that replacement remains unresolved on the inspected evidence. The project's AGPL-3.0-only license can cover its authored generator, metadata and artwork; it cannot relicense CairamR's source bytes or turn the whole replacement into project-owned material.

A lower-copy route is to distribute the project-authored source-pinned generator or a small change specification, letting each user transform their own installed 5.3 copy locally. A ready-made r2/r3 archive intended for public distribution calls for an author permission review covering the complete modified definition, plus review of the chosen host's terms. No author contact, public upload, or release is recorded by this assessment.
