# Free publication of the two stock-definition ATS mods

Inspected **2026-10-18**. Scope: free distribution of the ATS 1.61.3.18 **Job Types Compat Mustang** and **reasonable gas tank upgrades** packages generated from installed SCS stock data. This review does not cover the map fixes or any third-party map assets. It describes the primary published statements found, not a legal guarantee or a ruling on a later game/version, distribution platform, or account-specific upload agreement.

## What is in the current archives

**Observed locally — generated `.scs` ZIP entries inspected 2026-10-18:** Job Types Compat Mustang includes the complete 85,596-byte `def/car_job_data.sii` replacement with 58 `muscle` eligibility additions to the 149 stock units. The reasonable tank package includes complete `def/vehicle/car/ford.mustang_67/chassis/rwd.sii` (2,613 bytes) and `def/vehicle/car/ford.f150_23/chassis/4doors_55_awd.sii` (2,907 bytes), with only the two capacity-related fields changed in each. All three definitions are derived from SCS files, even though the edits are small. The archives also include project-authored metadata, descriptions, icons and audit reports. Neither archive includes extracted SCS vehicle models, textures or source artwork, or any third-party map file. The project icons depict Ford badges; their trademark presentation needs separate review before a public listing. Both manifests specify `dlc_dependencies[]: "dlc_rt_ford"` and `compatible_versions[]: "1.61.*"`; these are functional gates, not a license. Local source: `local_outputs/generated/2026-10-18_17-04-30/szczerbs-road-trip-job-types-compat-mustang-1.61-r3-experimental.scs` and `local_outputs/generated/2026-10-18_18-06-57/szczerbs-road-trip-reasonable-gas-tank-upgrades-1.61-r0-experimental.scs`. The exact edits and validation are in [the job generator record](PRACTICAL_COURIER_GENERATOR.md) and [the tank generator record](REASONABLE_GAS_TANK_UPGRADES.md).

## Publisher and platform statements

**Documented — SCS, 2019, still publicly available on inspection date:** SCS's [Important Notice For Our Modding Community](https://blog.scssoft.com/2019/04/important-notice.html) welcomes mods that adjust or improve its games, but rejects simply copying its content without meaningful changes or a DLC dependency for DLC content. The notice particularly addresses event rewards and Steam inventory items. It is therefore evidence of a conditional modding policy, not a blanket grant to redistribute extracted game assets.

**Documented — SCS staff clarification, 2019:** In the [thread's first post](https://forum.scssoft.com/viewtopic.php?t=270259), shown under Max with the role “SCS Software” and dated 5 April 2019, the short rule is “Don't make mods that bypass the need to buy content or earn it as a reward.” Its edit note says the poster was changed from Carthoo to Max to make the statement's official status clear. The post says to link to DLC data rather than put that content into a mod, then explicitly accepts a hypothetical mod changing DLC *definitions* (default paint colour) with no art assets and a Workshop DLC dependency: “no problem here.” It also says a mod manifest can specify DLC dependency. This is the closest found first-party answer to the two packages. It distinguishes altered definitions from copied models/textures, but does not explicitly discuss replacing a complete stock definition file after changing only a few fields, ATS 1.61 Road Trip: Ford, or `car_job_data.sii`. Treat the application of its example to these exact archives as an **inference**. Its general caveat against copied DLC content should remain visible alongside the permissive definition example.

**Documented — SCS technical support, inspected 2026-10-18:** The [official game archive extractor guide](https://modding.scssoft.com/wiki/Documentation/Tools/Game_Archive_Extractor) permits unpacking the game archive to develop mods. The [mod manager documentation](https://modding.scssoft.com/wiki/Documentation/Engine/Mod_manager) recommends ZIP archives for releases and documents `dlc_dependencies[]` as blocking activation when required DLC is missing; it also documents DLC-gated mod subpackages. SCS's [Workshop uploader guide](https://modding.scssoft.com/wiki/Tutorials/SCS_Workshop_Uploader/How_to_upload_new_mod%3F) gives an upload workflow and says the Steam Workshop terms must be accepted. These technical instructions support the *mechanics* of building and sharing mods; by themselves they do not grant rights to every file extracted from a game or DLC.

**Documented — Valve, Steam Subscriber Agreement, current page inspected 2026-10-18:** [Section 6.B](https://store.steampowered.com/subscriber_agreement/#6) explicitly contemplates Workshop contributions based on or using a Workshop-enabled game and says they are in principle free. [Section 6.A and 6.D](https://store.steampowered.com/subscriber_agreement/#6) require the uploader to grant Valve distribution and related rights, warrant sufficient rights in all uploaded content, warrant original creation by the contributor(s), and warrant that submission violates no contract or law. Section 6.B permits app-specific terms and Valve to restrict or remove Workshop items. This places the rights assessment on the uploader; the existence of an ATS Workshop or acceptance by an uploader is not clearance of copied definitions. [ATS's Workshop landing page](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/about/?appid=270880) advertises fan-created mods. The [general Workshop information page](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/workshopsubmitinfo/) says acceptable items and rules differ by product and submission accepts the applicable legal agreement.

**Unknown — account-specific and game-specific terms:** The public [ATS Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/270880/American_Truck_Simulator/) inspected here showed Workshop support but did not expose an ATS-specific EULA link. Direct public retrieval of `https://store.steampowered.com/eula/270880` failed; a read-only scan of the local ATS installation found only `BulletLicense.txt` and `Xiph.Org-license-bsd.txt` under `licenses/`, not an ATS EULA. This does **not** prove there is no game-specific agreement. The actual logged-in Steam Workshop acceptance screen and any terms applicable to a future hosting site were not inspected. Check them at publication time; if they conflict with the 2019 SCS clarification, do not assume the older post overrides them.

**Documented — forum only:** [SCS's Mods forum rule](https://forum.scssoft.com/viewforum.php?f=24) limits posts in that forum to free public mods and support for mods. It does not by itself set the rules for Steam Workshop or every other distribution site. Both proposed releases are free, so this particular forum rule would not exclude them.

## Application to these two packages

| Package | Supported reading of SCS's published examples | Specific residual uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job Types Compat Mustang | **Inference:** A gameplay-relevant 58-job eligibility edit to SCS stock definitions, with Ford DLC dependency and no art assets, fits the broad pattern of an SCS-accepted definition mod. It does not provide paid DLC content to non-owners. | The replacement contains the *entire* 149-unit stock `car_job_data.sii`, most of it unchanged. No found SCS statement expressly evaluates wholesale `car_job_data.sii` replacement, and the Steam rights warranty still applies on Workshop. |
| Reasonable gas tank upgrades | **Inference:** The DLC-gated, art-free edits to two Ford chassis definitions are particularly close to SCS's accepted example of changing a small property in DLC definitions. The game still supplies the cars and their art. | Each complete chassis file retains almost all original bytes and changes only `tank_size` and matching `info[]`. SCS did not expressly approve these exact Ford files or quantify when a copied definition has enough modification. |

**Practical recommendation, 2026-10-18:** There is a concrete publisher statement favoring *free, DLC-dependent, art-free definition edits*, so the record does not support a categorical claim that these two archives may never be shared. Conversely, technical modding support and the 2019 example are not an unconditional redistribution license. If sharing these archives for community testing, keep them free, keep the `dlc_rt_ford` dependency, state ATS 1.61 and the exact resource scope, avoid any extracted stock models/textures or third-party files, review the Ford badge presentation in the project icons, and review the chosen host's upload terms before posting. If the user needs explicit clearance for the complete-file implementation or a particular Workshop warranty, ask SCS about these exact three files and their DLC gate; the answer is currently **Unknown**. The existing generators already make these edits from a player's installed game and DLC when that player runs them locally, which avoids distributing the generated `.scs` with copied stock bytes. A separately distributable binary delta or simplified end-user patch workflow has not been implemented or tested here.
